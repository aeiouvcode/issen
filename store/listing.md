## Full description
ISSEN is a sword duel painted in wet ink. One arena, one blade, and an opponent who lies to you: a strike with no ring on the ground is a feint. Read the windup, dodge at the last breath to turn their cut aside, and answer with a single clean stroke.

Three chapters, three masters:
- Chapter one - the shade walks alone. Learn the read.
- Chapter two - the lancer takes the field, faster and sharper.
- Chapter three - the iron hall. The full duel, no mercy.

Earn stances as you go: the iai draw starts your first cut already drawn, and the iron tetsu stance trades speed for crushing posture damage. Perfect reads build a streak; finishers end a broken opponent in one brushstroke.

Built as ink on paper: every splatter, ghost and ribbon is drawn in code. The sound is synthesized the same way - softened shamisen and taiko, quiet by design. No ads, no accounts, works offline once loaded.
