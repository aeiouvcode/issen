## Short description (80 max)
A sumi-e sword duel. Read the blade, parry, and cut through a thousand layers.

## Full description
ISSEN is a hand-inked sword duel on a parchment field. Every figure is brushwork that boils like hand animation; every cut throws ink across the grass.

- Read the blade. A red ink star flashes the instant a foe's swing can be parried. Cut on it and the foe staggers open for a full-power riposte. Cut at the very last moment for a perfect parry.
- Foes that fight back differently. Straw-hat ronin with long blades, armored ronin whose lacquer plates must be split first, and spear ronin who guard their front with the shaft and punish a straight approach.
- A boss for every chapter. Kageyama waits in the grass. Plain cuts barely move him; parry, riposte, and survive his second breath.
- Chapter map and blade stances. Clear chapters to learn Tsubame, the quick swallow cut, and Iwa, the heavy stone cut that splits armor.
- Sound made in code. Soft taiko and shamisen that swell with the fight, and quiet blade sounds with no harsh highs.
- Touch, keyboard, mouse and gamepad.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No data collected.
